# IEEE Venue Candidates: BRPHM Cross-Orbit RUL

**Access date:** 2026-10-02. **Purpose:** shortlist, not venue confirmation or an acceptance assessment. Paper evidence: [F1 closeout](f1_closeout_20261002.md) and current [English manuscript](../en/main.tex). No manuscript files were edited for this note.

## Recommendation

1. **IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement (TIM)** - best conditional fit if the paper is reframed around a controlled measurement-derived diagnostic/evaluation protocol and its limits. TIM's official scope is explicitly measurement science, methods, functionality, and applications. The current work uses measured/simulated telemetry but contributes mainly a source-only RUL model-selection protocol; a model comparison alone may not establish an instrumentation-and-measurement contribution. The official page warns that scope mismatch can trigger immediate screening rejection.
2. **IEEE Transactions on Reliability (T-Rel)** - strong topical fit for RUL credibility, reliability assessment, and source-only generalization, but less direct for a paper whose current outcome is a deterministic error gate rather than reliability/uncertainty analysis. Its official journal and IEEE author pages were not readable in this environment, so scope and submission constraints remain provisional here.
3. **IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems (TAES)** - clearest application-domain fit for spacecraft components and telemetry, with recent spacecraft RUL precedent. It is a weaker fit for the current narrow simulator result: the new LEO600 partition has only 5 usable BAT units and 1 RWA observation, and the simulator/model orbit-period mismatch is about 79 seconds (about 608 km). Use this candidate only if the aerospace-system contribution is made central and those bounds are prominent.

This ranking is for a **controlled diagnostic/protocol or measurement/RUL paper**, not a claim that the current draft is ready to submit. If the paper remains primarily a predictive-method paper, TIM's scope risk increases; T-Rel may move up only if the reliability/credibility contribution is developed. The existing comparisons below establish topic precedent, not novelty, fair-baseline status, or scope acceptance.
